FAQs, terms & conditions

Please review the estate details and purchase conditions carefully.

Where is the estate located?

Gwagwalada, Abuja.

Who owns and develops the estate?

Gtext Lands.

What type of title do you have on the land?

Certificate of Occupancy.

Are there any encumbrances on the land?

The land is free from government acquisition and adverse claims.

What is the size of a plot?

400 sqm.

What other payment is required apart from payment for the land?

No further payments are required except those necessary for perfection of title, which may vary based on statutory fees imposed by the relevant government authority. In addition, the purchaser shall pay developmental fees of $8,333.33 (₦12,500,000), which can be spread over 12 to 36 months, representing charges for estate improvements and other developmental obligations during the land banking period. Infrastructure fees or service charges may also be determined at the time of construction on the purchased plot(s).

What infrastructure is expected?

Drainage systems, electrification and street lighting, water supply, waste management, security cameras, paved roads and perimeter fencing.

What do I get after the initial payment?

A receipt for the payments made.

Is the road to the estate accessible?

Yes, the road to the estate is accessible.

When will my plot(s) be allocated?

Your plots will be allocated as soon as payment for the plot has been made in full. Upon purchase, a temporary allocation will be assigned and sent to you. You then have 3 months to complete payment for the land, after which your allocation will be made permanent.

What is the size of a corner piece and how much does it cost?

The size varies; the amount will be prorated using the amount per square meter at the point of purchase.

Can I start construction immediately?

Construction can commence once payment for the land is complete and the survey, title deeds and building approval have been obtained.

Are commercial plots for sale?

Yes.

Are there restrictions on the type of building I can construct?

All building designs must be submitted to Gtext Land, approved by Gtext Homes in the first instance, and then by the Federal Capital Territory Ministry of Physical Planning and Urban Development before building work can commence.

Can I have a survey for more than one plot?

Yes. More than one survey is possible, but it attracts additional costs.

Can I pay an agent?

No. All payments should be made to the Gtext Lands account at its designated banks. Cheques should be issued in favour of Gtext Land. The company shall not accept responsibility for any liability resulting from a deviation from this instruction.

What if I cannot continue with payments? Can I get a refund?

Please refer to the refund policy below.

Can I resell my plot or property?

Yes. Subscribers who are up to date on land payment, documentation levy and development levy can resell their plots, subject to approval by Gtext Land management. The re-seller must provide the buyer's details to the company so its database can be updated.

Purchase conditions

Refund policy

The Company shall not make any refunds for lands purchased. All land purchases completed and confirmed by Gtext Land Limited are final and non-refundable.

Once a purchase has been concluded and the land allocated to the purchaser, payments made shall not be refunded merely because the purchaser changes their mind, decides not to proceed with the property, or is no longer interested in the purchase.

Purchasers are advised to conduct all necessary inspections to ensure they are satisfied with the property and its terms before completing payment.
